App Privacy Disclosures

A plain summary of the data categories WEEKLY collects and the ones it does not.

Effective August 5, 2026 Updated September 11, 2026 Version 2026-09-11

Data linked to the user

  • Contact Info: profile name and email from authentication when provided.
  • Identifiers: account and provider IDs, device identifier, and push token for notification delivery.
  • User Content: interests, profile prompt answers, legacy bio data retained from earlier versions, profile photos, availability, plans, chats, chat photos, voice messages, and safety reports. Date of birth is used for age eligibility; a gender selection is required during onboarding and used for discovery filters.
  • Location (Precise): the app requests when-in-use location and reads device GPS to set your city and coordinates and to suggest nearby places for plans. City and coordinates plus place search data are stored.
  • Usage Data: requests, connections, plans, meeting confirmations, and notification read states stored to operate the app. Moments is currently disabled.
  • Operational records: server request and error logs maintained by our hosting providers. The app has no configured third-party crash or analytics SDK; development console logging is not a production analytics service.

Not collected in current code

  • Contacts address book.
  • Payment information.
  • Advertising ID or cross-app tracking data.
  • Third-party advertising or third-party analytics SDK data.

Questions
