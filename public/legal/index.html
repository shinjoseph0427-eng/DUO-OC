Safety

WEEKLY is an 18+ app. You can report or block anyone from their profile or a chat. Reports go to a moderation queue that only server-verified moderators can review. WEEKLY reviews reports promptly and prioritizes reports based on severity, safety risk, and available evidence. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

You can delete your account and its personal data at any time from Settings. You can reach WEEKLY for support, privacy questions, or to exercise your data rights at weeklysupport@gmail.com.
