Community Guidelines

Effective August 5, 2026 Updated September 12, 2026 Version 2026-09-12

The standard

WEEKLY is for adults making real plans with real people. Be honest, respectful, and careful with other people in the app and offline.

Not allowed

  • Minors or attempts to involve minors.
  • Harassment, threats, bullying, hate, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual behavior.
  • Impersonation, fake profiles, scams, spam, phishing, or attempts to collect sensitive information.
  • Nudity, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, or sexual content involving minors.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), grooming, sextortion, or trafficking of children.
  • Illegal activity, weapons sales, drug sales, fraud, or instructions for harm.
  • Sharing someone else private information without permission.
  • Evading a block, ban, or moderation action.

Reporting and blocking

Use Report or Block when something feels wrong. A blocked user is restricted by server-side rules from connecting, messaging, and appearing in relevant areas. Reports may include the reason, details, and context so WEEKLY can review abuse.

WEEKLY uses rule-based checks before supported user text is posted or sent, plus user reports, blocking, and human review. Reported content goes to a moderation queue that only server-verified moderators can review. WEEKLY reviews reports promptly and prioritizes based on severity, safety risk, and available evidence. WEEKLY may remove the content, remove the account responsible, or both. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. You can also email reports and safety concerns to weeklysupport@gmail.com.

Non-consensual intimate imagery

WEEKLY does not allow intimate images shared without the consent of the person shown. If an intimate image of you was shared without your consent, report it in the app or email weeklysupport@gmail.com with enough detail to identify the content. WEEKLY removes content confirmed to be non-consensual intimate imagery, and any known copies of it, within 48 hours of receiving a valid request, and may remove the account responsible.

You do not need an account to make this request. If you cannot reach the content in the app, email weeklysupport@gmail.com and describe where it appeared.

Meeting in person

Meet in public, keep your own transportation options, do not share sensitive personal information too early, and leave if a situation feels off. WEEKLY cannot guarantee another person identity, intentions, or behavior.
