Delete your WEEKLY account

In the app, open Settings > Delete Account and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email weeklysupport@gmail.com from the email associated with your WEEKLY account, with the subject WEEKLY account deletion. Include your username so we can locate your account. If you cannot access that email, contact support for an alternative ownership check. Never send your password or a verification code.

After verifying ownership, we delete your sign-in account, profile, photos, messages, and other account-owned data. Backups expire on their normal rotation; records needed for legal obligations or an active safety investigation may be retained as described in our Privacy Policy.
