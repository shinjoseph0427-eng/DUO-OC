WEEKLY policy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026 Updated September 11, 2026 Version 2026-09-11

Overview

WEEKLY helps adults make real-life plans around shared interests and activities, with profiles, weekly availability, requests, connections, and chat. This policy describes the data WEEKLY collects and how it is used.

This policy is based on the current app code and Supabase backend. WEEKLY does not currently use advertising identifiers, third-party ad SDKs, or a third-party analytics SDK.

Information you provide

  • Authentication data from Apple or Google, including provider account identifiers and email address when provided by the provider.
  • Profile data, including name, username, date of birth or birth year for age checks, city, coordinates (which may be precise when you use your current location), time zone, profile prompt answers, legacy bio data retained from earlier versions, gender selection required during onboarding (including a prefer-not-to-say option, used for discovery filters), interests, optional social handles, and profile photos.
  • Weekly availability and planning details, including days, times, places, activities, requests, connections, and plans.
  • User content, including profile photos, activity labels, place tags, and chat messages, photos, and voice messages.
  • Chat media, including images and voice messages sent to accepted connections.
  • Safety data, including reports, block actions, report reasons, report details, and the reported context when provided.

Permissions and device data

Camera and photo library access are used when you add profile photos or send chat images. Microphone access is used for voice messages. Location access is used to help set your city and find nearby plan suggestions. You can decline location access and type a city manually.

If you enable push notifications, WEEKLY stores an Expo push token, device identifier, platform, and app version so notifications can be delivered and disabled when you sign out.

How WEEKLY uses information

  • Create, authenticate, and secure your account.
  • Show your profile, interests, and availability for activity discovery.
  • Enable requests, accepted connections, chats, plans, and notifications.
  • Operate safety features such as reporting, blocking, and abuse review.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce WEEKLY policies.

Sharing and processors

Your profile and relevant availability may be shown to other WEEKLY users. Chat content is shown only according to the app permissions and connection rules in the product. Profile photos, chat images, and voice messages are held for moderator review before they are available to others. Authorized moderators can review media and content reported for safety concerns.

WEEKLY uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and Edge Functions; Apple and Google for sign-in and platform services; Expo for push notification delivery; and Google Places through a server-side proxy for location search and nearby place suggestions.

WEEKLY does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising based on the current codebase.

Retention and deletion

Account, profile, plan, connection, chat, notification, and safety data is retained while the account is active unless deleted earlier by product behavior.

You can delete your account in Settings. Deleting your account removes your sign-in identity, removes the database rows you own, and removes your stored profile and chat media. Apple authorization is revoked for accounts linked to Sign in with Apple; you may be asked to authenticate with Apple again to complete this step.

Encrypted backups may retain copies of deleted data for a limited period before they expire on their normal rotation. WEEKLY may also retain records for longer where required by law or to resolve a safety or abuse investigation. You can reach WEEKLY for support, privacy questions, or to exercise your data rights at weeklysupport@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings.
  • Block or report users from supported profile and chat contexts.
  • Turn off location, camera, photo, microphone, or notification permissions in device settings.
  • Use Apple private relay email when supported by Sign in with Apple.

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information. You can reach WEEKLY for support, privacy questions, or to exercise your data rights at weeklysupport@gmail.com.

Children

WEEKLY is only for adults 18 and older. The app requires an age check during onboarding and is not intended for children or minors.

Security

WEEKLY uses Supabase authentication, row-level access controls, server-side functions, and private storage with signed URLs for sensitive media. No app or network service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contact

You can reach WEEKLY for support, privacy questions, or to exercise your data rights at weeklysupport@gmail.com.
